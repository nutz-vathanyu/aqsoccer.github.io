Privacy Policy

AQsoccer - Aquarium Soccer 3D: Solo & Online PvP
Developer: Vathanyu Studio · Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Contact: nutz.vty@gmail.com

Vathanyu Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates AQsoccer - Aquarium Soccer 3D: Solo & Online PvP (“AQsoccer” or the “Game”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the Game, why it is used, and the choices and rights that may be available to you.

AQsoccer does not require a conventional account with a username, email address, or password. The Game does use a generated UUID and online services. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, that consent will be requested through the Game, the relevant service, or your device.

1. Information We Process

UUID and game profile

The Game uses a generated unique identifier (UUID) associated with a game installation or device-associated profile. The UUID and related records are stored on servers used to operate the Game. We use them to identify the game profile, associate item catalog data and digital entitlements with it, support game features, and help protect the service. Although the UUID is not a name or email address, it may be personal data if it can be linked to an identifiable person or device.

Catalog, gameplay, and digital item records

We process item identifiers, catalog records, unlocked items, entitlements, and related game-profile information needed to provide the Game and maintain the items available to a profile.

In-app purchase records

Google Play processes payments through Google Play Billing. The Game or its server may receive or process product identifiers, purchase tokens or transaction references, purchase status, and entitlement information to verify purchases and provide or restore eligible products. We do not receive your full payment card details through the Game’s own purchase system. Google’s handling of payment information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Online PvP and technical information

When you use online PvP, information needed to establish and operate sessions may be processed by our servers or multiplayer providers. Depending on the implementation, this may include session identifiers, match and synchronization data, connection information, IP address, device and app diagnostics, and event timestamps. We use this information to run multiplayer, maintain reliability, troubleshoot problems, and protect the Game from abuse.

Unity Ads

AQsoccer uses Unity Ads to show interstitial advertisements. Unity and its advertising partners may process advertising or device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and technical information, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, and other advertising-related app activity. Their processing can support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and—where permitted—personalized advertising. See Unity’s privacy policy for game players and app users. The exact data processed and available consent choices depend on the SDK configuration, your region, device settings, and Unity’s current practices.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain Game features, catalog content, and online PvP.
  • Associate game records and digital items with a game profile.
  • Verify eligible purchases and entitlements.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, and investigate technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.
  • Show and measure interstitial advertisements through Unity Ads.

3. Legal Bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely as appropriate on providing the Game or a requested feature, our legitimate interests in security and reliable operations, compliance with legal obligations, or your consent for processing that requires it. You may withdraw consent through the applicable privacy controls, where available; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

We share or make information available to service providers only as needed to operate or support the Game, subject to their roles and applicable requirements. These may include:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing for payments, purchase records, and related services (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Unity Ads for advertising and related measurement or fraud prevention (Unity privacy policy).
  • Hosting and multiplayer providers that store UUID-associated records or support game servers, sessions, and online play.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the Game, or as part of a lawful business transaction, subject to applicable law.

5. Advertising Choices

Interstitial ads may still appear if personalized advertising is disabled. Depending on your location and the Game’s SDK configuration, you may be able to manage consent or ad personalization through an in-game consent prompt, Unity’s available controls, or your device settings. Review Unity’s privacy policy for current information.

6. Storage, Security, and Retention

UUID-associated game information is stored on servers used to operate AQsoccer. The hosting locations and countries where information is processed depend on the providers we use. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Game, maintain valid entitlements, meet legal requirements, address support issues, or protect the service. When it is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it according to our procedures. Third-party providers may apply their own retention periods under their policies.

7. Your Requests and Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, request portability, or complain to a data protection authority.

To make a privacy or deletion request, email nutz.vty@gmail.com with the subject “AQsoccer – Privacy / Data Deletion Request.” Include your game UUID if available so we can locate the relevant records. We may ask for reasonable verification. Deleting game records may remove progress or virtual items and may affect purchase restoration. We may retain limited information where law permits or requires it, such as for legal, financial, or fraud-prevention purposes. Data controlled separately by third parties may need to be managed through those providers.

8. Children’s Privacy

AQsoccer is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit names, email addresses, or other directly identifying details through an account registration system. If you believe a child’s information has been processed in a way that violates applicable law, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. International Processing

Our service providers may process information outside your country. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Game, our data practices, providers, or applicable law. We will post the revised policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.
